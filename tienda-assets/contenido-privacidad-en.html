This is a translation of our privacy policy in Spanish. If the two versions differ, the Spanish version prevails.

At Astrea Charts we take the privacy of your data seriously. This document explains clearly what information we collect, what we use it for, and what rights you have over it.

1. What data we collect

When you generate your birth chart (free or premium), we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Date, time and place of birth (city and country)

This data is strictly necessary to calculate your astrological chart accurately — without the date, time and place of birth, the astronomical calculation is not possible.

2. What we use your data for

  • To calculate the planetary positions and generate your birth chart.
  • To generate the written interpretation of your report (free or premium).
  • To send you your premium report by email, if you buy it.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it or share it with third parties for marketing.

3. Who we share information with

To provide the service, some data passes through external providers, solely for the technical purpose of generating your chart:

  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim — converts your city and country into geographic coordinates.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — helps write the interpretation of your chart, based on the calculated astrological data (signs, houses, aspects). We do not share your name or your email address with it. The generated text is not sent as is: our team reviews it manually, checks it against reference tools (AstroSeek) and complements it with the writing of transits before delivering your report.
  • Hotmart — processes the payment for the premium report; we never see or store your card information.

4. Where and how your data is stored

Your data is stored securely on our infrastructure (Railway), with restricted access. We keep the calculation of your chart so we don't have to recalculate it if you look at it again, but we do not share this information with anyone outside the providers mentioned above.

5. Your rights

In accordance with Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012 (Personal Data Protection Law) and the data protection laws that may apply depending on your country of residence, you have the right to:

  • Know, update and correct your personal data.
  • Request the deletion of your data from our records.
  • Revoke the authorization you gave for the processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at guiasastrologicas@gmail.com and we will handle your request as soon as possible.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make important changes, we will show it by updating the date at the beginning of this document.
