Frequently asked questions

Any questions?

What is a birth chart?

It’s a map of the sky calculated for the exact date, time and place of your birth. It shows where the Sun, the Moon and the planets were, and how they relate to each other and to the areas of your life.

How accurate is the reading?

Positions are calculated with real astronomy (Swiss Ephemeris), not generic tables. The interpretation is written with the help of AI assistants and then we review each one before sending it. It’s a tool for self-discovery, not a prediction.

How long does it take to arrive?

Between 24 and 48 hours after we confirm your payment, straight to your inbox.

In what format do I receive the reading?

As a PDF, with an editorial design made to be read slowly on your phone or computer, or printed. Your reading is written in Spanish.

Do I need to know my time of birth?

It’s ideal, because your Ascendant and houses depend on it. If you don’t know it exactly, use the closest time you have: most sections are still useful.

Do you offer transit or solar return readings?

Your full reading already includes a chapter on today’s sky and the coming months. For your year ahead, there is a dedicated Solar return reading in the shop.
